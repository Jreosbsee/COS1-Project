Video game idea.

Player will travel through a map collecting items, coins, and artifacts related to the story.

~~~Maps~~~ 
- I'll start out with 1 "map" outline its difficulty, and then going on from there. I'll use ASCII to create the layout of the map, and make different rooms and interactive items.
In full, I'd like to have at least 5 maps. If I stick to this, we'll have 4 maps that have an artifact and loot in each one, and then one map with a "boss" that gives OP item(s) to the
player. It'd be cool if the player could replay the maps with the OP items equipped (just for the fun of causing chaos through the second playthrough)

~~~Items~~~ 
- Coins: There will be a coin system that allows the player to collect coins while traveling through the map. If I have time, I'd like to add a shop where the players can buy and sell items.
- Armor: Armor will be obtained from defeating enemies or from the shop (if shop is implemented). Ideally, armor will be better as the player travel through the maps.

~~~Weapons~~~ 
- Player will start out with a default weapon. Other weapons will be found via defeating enemies or buying from the shop (if the shop is implemented).
- Weapons will only consist of swords (or melee). Thinking of swords and daggers as the extent of this.
- Weapons will deal different damage levels. Ideally, the weapons will be stronger as the player travel through the maps.




Ideas to potentially imeplement later:
- Difficulty (easy on default, and then a medium and hard mode)
- Shop for coins


Important things to work on:
- Learning and implemnting ASCII.
- Figuring out visual maps for the player to walk on and interact with.
